What we champion

Who we are

Built for leadership, advocacy, and action.

Kaya Natin! Youth — Candelaria brings together young people who believe that honest leadership and collective service can transform communities.

Mission

To empower youth in Candelaria to participate meaningfully in civic life, strengthen ethical leadership, and support programs that improve the well-being of families and communities.

Vision

To cultivate a generation of responsible, compassionate, and active young leaders who embody integrity, transparency, and service in every community they serve.
